How the hour runs

Same four steps, every session.

Structure is the point. An unstructured hour becomes a venting session; this one ends with a number you're willing to defend and the words you'll use to defend it.

01

Position

Fifteen minutes on the facts — the property, the comps, the timeline, and what you can and can't afford to lose.

02

Score

We fill the scorecard together, out loud. Most people discover they have more leverage than they thought, in a place they weren't looking.

03

Rehearse

I play the other side. You practice the anchor, the silence, and the counter until they stop feeling awkward to say.

04

Write it down

The plan gets committed to paper while it's fresh — anchor, ladder, walk-away, and the exact sentence you lead with.

Questions

The four things everyone asks.

How does the credit-back at closing work?

If you transact with me — list with me as your seller's agent, or close a purchase with me as your buyer's agent — the full session fee is credited back to you on the closing statement. If you go with someone else, you keep the scorecard, the strategy, and the recap PDF, and the fee stands as paid.

Why is this paid when every other agent's consultation is free?

Because a free consultation is a sales call, and both of us know it. Charging for the hour means it gets spent on your deal instead of on a pitch — and an agent who discounts their own fee before the work starts is telling you what they'll do when it's your money on the table.

Do I need to have a house picked out already?

No. Buyers get the most out of this before their first offer, and sellers before the listing goes live. If you do have a specific address in play, bring it — the scorecard is sharper with a real property in front of it.

Is this available outside Illinois?

The coaching is, anywhere — strategy isn't a licensed activity. Representation is Illinois only. Outside Illinois the session ends with a referral to a licensed partner broker in your state, and the credit-back doesn't apply, since I'm not the agent of record.
