FAQ

Straight answers.

What do you actually do — list the house, or run the build?

You build. I'm the broker. I help with land and infill sourcing, pricing, buyer demand, and selling the finished homes. I don't pour the foundation, and I don't pretend I do.

Where do you work?

Around Joliet, Illinois, and the Chicago suburbs. If the lot is outside that, send it anyway. I'll tell you if I can help.

Will you only sell to trades buyers?

No. I will not prefer, steer, or exclude a buyer. Households in the trades — electricians, plumbers, linemen, union trades — are a big share of who buys new construction in this market, and I already have reach with them. That describes an audience I can talk to. It is not a rule about who may buy.
