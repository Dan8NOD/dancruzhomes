Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 November 2026 · Applies to dancruzhomes.com and the Dan Cruz Homes app for iPhone and iPad

Dan Cruz Homes is run by Daniel Cruz under Fat Cat Asset Management, and is licensed under the brokerage Westward360. This policy covers the website, the Property Readiness Grader, and the Dan Cruz Homes app. You have an account so that the properties you grade follow you between the app and the website. We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for targeted advertising.

The short version
  • We collect your email address and the properties, scorecards, notes and photos you add.
  • We never sell or rent personal information.
  • If you agree to it when you start the grader on the website, we send you occasional emails about Dan Cruz Homes and related Fat Cat Asset Management services (coaching, NOD tools). Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • There are no advertising trackers, no ad IDs and no third-party marketing pixels, in the app or on the site.
  • We never see your card number. Stripe and Apple handle payment details.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app: Account → Delete my account. Or email hello@negotiatorsondemand.com.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising or tracking IDs. The app doesn't use the Advertising Identifier or ask for tracking permission, because it doesn't track you.
  • No card numbers, CVVs or bank details. Those go straight to Stripe or Apple.
  • No contacts, microphone, location or health data.

Sharing a property

Every saved property has a private share link. Anyone you give the link to can see that property's scorecard, read-only, on dancruzhomes.com. If you've subscribed to the portal, Dan Cruz can see your properties so he can give you feedback. That feedback is the service you're paying for.

Who else handles your data

We only use companies that do a specific job for the product, and none of them may use your information for their own marketing.

  • Supabase: accounts, sign-in, the database, and photo storage.
  • Vercel: website hosting.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, notifications, and purchases inside the app. Apple only tells us that a valid subscription exists and when it ends.
  • Google: Google sign-in, if you choose it.
  • Stripe: payments on the website.
  • Kit (ConvertKit): email, for people who agreed to receive it.

We will also disclose information where the law requires it. If Dan Cruz Homes is ever sold or merged, your data may transfer with it, and this policy will still apply to it.

Subscriptions bought in the app

When you subscribe inside the app, Apple takes the payment and gives the app a signed receipt. The app sends that receipt to our server, which checks it with Apple and records that your account is subscribed. That's why a purchase on your iPhone also unlocks the portal when you sign in at dancruzhomes.com with the same account. We never receive your payment method, your Apple Account name or your billing address.

How long we keep it

  • Account, properties and photos: until you delete them or your account.
  • Payment and subscription records: as long as tax and accounting law requires, usually seven years, even after the account is closed.
  • Support email: while it helps us sort out your issue, then deleted.

Your choices

  • Delete it. In the app, go to Account → Delete my account. That removes your properties, photos and scorecards everywhere, including on the website. Deleting your account doesn't cancel an App Store subscription. Cancel that in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.
  • See it, correct it, or take a copy. Email us and we'll send you what we hold, fix what's wrong, or send you a machine-readable export.

Email hello@negotiatorsondemand.com for any of these. We aim to reply within 30 days. In the EEA or UK, these are your GDPR rights of access, rectification, erasure and portability. In California, they are your CCPA rights to know, delete and correct. We don't sell or share personal information as those laws define it, so there is nothing to opt out of.

Children

Dan Cruz Homes is for adults buying or selling property. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child has made an account, tell us and we'll remove it.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit and passwords are hashed. Database access is restricted so each account can only reach its own rows. The app keeps your sign-in in the iOS Keychain, and our server checks payment receipts instead of trusting the device. No system is perfect. If we ever find a breach that affects your data, we will tell you.

International transfers

Dan Cruz Homes operates from the United States, and the services above process data there.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date at the top and say so in the app or on the site.
